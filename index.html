UFAQS

A system that lets NU Dasmariñas students book campus facilities and line up for school offices like the Registrar, Treasury and ITSO from their phone, a kiosk or the web.

Project Manager Full-stack Developer Team of three In progress

What I did

  • Lead a team of three: plan the work, split tasks, and keep the build moving toward our capstone defense.
  • Built the web portals for students, approvers, librarians and admins, plus the student mobile app.
  • Set up the database behind room bookings, approval steps, queue tickets and user roles.
  • Built a machine learning model that predicts how long a student will wait in line, using past queue data. The estimate shows in the app and on the kiosk.
  • Added live queue updates, push notifications and SMS alerts, and fixed security gaps that let people without an account read or change queue data.

Built with

FlutterDartSupabasePostgreSQLPythonscikit-learnFastAPIFirebaseGoogle Cloud RunDocker

Who uses it

Students and faculty book rooms and join queues. Program chairs, department endorsers, the facility office and librarians approve bookings. Office staff call tickets at their windows, and admins manage users and settings.

Q-03Team project2025

QuickQ

A queue system for the NU Dasmariñas Treasury Office. Students take a QR ticket at a kiosk, follow their place in line on their phone, and get an alert when it's almost their turn.

Backend Developer Team project Finished

What I did

  • Built the server and database for the ticket kiosk, the student queue tracker, and the staff and admin screens.
  • Made the queue update on everyone's screen right away when a ticket is called.
  • Sent students a phone notification or SMS when their turn was close.
  • Secured staff and kiosk logins and kept a record of every staff action.

Built with

Node.jsExpressPostgreSQLKnexWeb PushSemaphore SMSDockerCloudflare Tunnel

The two-minute rule

When a window calls a ticket, the student has a grace period (two minutes by default) to show up. If they don't, staff mark a no-show and the line keeps moving.
